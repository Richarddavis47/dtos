## Evidence contract

Prices must match the shared canonical Market balance admitted in each completed
evaluation and its current request's neutral asset pool. Full compatible prices
on both sides, valid exact identities and canonical ownership are required for
cost selection. Missing, partial, nonfinite, negative, incompatible-unit,
changed-price or ambiguous pick evidence cannot win cost selection. Zero remains
zero. A missing cost never removes a separately eligible assessed offer; its cost
is unknown. Incoming extras and net received-minus-sent are separate, using the
same canonical package arithmetic. Different extras are not equivalent purchases.

Custom construction and evaluation preserve their existing saved method,
revision, scope and prices. Cost selection uses the preserved neutral pools and
neutral evaluator evidence, never personal slot values. Same-search source
boundary and ownership rechecks remain in place. No retained offer is reranked
against a later inventory or quote. Existing stale/retained and comparison
admission checks govern read-only previews and reload/Back.

Cost roles are descriptions within a limited search, not global optima, manager
asking prices, acceptance promises, new recommendation tiers or valuations.
FAIR / OPTIONAL remains optional, and cheaper rejected/evidence-limited offers
remain outside eligible results. Exact target, original-franchise picks,
protections and exclusions remain enforced by the existing search and evaluator.
