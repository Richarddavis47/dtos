## Contract

The existing canonical directed-movement reader admits consideration independently for players and exact picks. Each completed transaction is counted once per source league/transaction identity. Conflicting versions, malformed transactions, pending/rejected/cancelled/failed records, waivers, corrections and unlinked franchise observations do not become completed trade activity. A repeated asset in two real deals appears in both gross directions; net is only acquisitions minus disposals **in the observed trade sample**, never current holdings or complete franchise progress. Incomplete consideration preserves supported lower bounds and withholds the affected net. An observed pick conversion remains a draft event, not an additional trade acquisition.

Source-season selection uses retained authoritative season identities. It is not a guessed date range; supported completion dates describe only the dated subset. Missing periods remain unknown. A zero for an empty selected period requires complete source coverage and supported franchise continuity. Known zero transfers within an observed nonempty sample do not establish zero activity in unacquired periods. A source version change withholds every numeric summary.

Exact pick identities retain draft year, round and original source roster. “Own” refers to that original franchise in the source-linked chain, not today's owner or a manager's tenure. Historical event labels reuse source-season identities. Existing subsequent-trade, package, pick-conversion and contextual Back paths are retained. Manager changes remain season observations and are not backdated to transaction actors. Current values, projections and ownership are not used to supply historical facts.
