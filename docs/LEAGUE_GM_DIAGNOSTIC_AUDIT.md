## Small shared implementation

Extend the existing `gm_behavioral_intelligence` package with a disposable observation report over canonical Sleeper prepared transaction sections and existing ownership evidence. Independent completed transaction identities establish samples; directed original assets establish composition. Do not read current prices as historical value or transfer franchise history to a current owner. Historical ages without transaction-time support remain unavailable. Season-observed owner attribution must say it does not establish exact intra-season takeover time; current ownership alone does not backdate tenure.

Coverage uses existing transaction bucket states (observed, observed-empty, unavailable, unacquired, malformed). Complete acquisition is scoped to the observed season/periods, never proof of all league history. Unknown completion clocks still support completed-event counts, but not dated frequency/trends. Duplicate conflicts are withheld. Multi-party trades remain one event and directed FAAB is not a priced player/pick.

One bounded process-local report cache is keyed by league, factual source/identity revision and methodology; no permanent derived archive. Retrieval-only changes do not change semantic identity. Current roster needs and strategy are joined at consumption, not manufactured from history. Cold archive decoding/memory and Trade latency will be measured; Home must stay prepared-only. Resource gates remain 2 GiB and 500 ms.

## Implemented evidence and admission contract

- Report at `gm_behavioral_intelligence.diagnostics`: completed status plus valid transaction/participant identity; deduplicate per source-league transaction, withhold conflicting copies. Counts remain independent decisions; exact original-franchise picks and directed FAAB are separate consideration. Unsupported asset directions qualify composition rather than inventing zeros.
- Observations are capped at 10,000 source rows / latest 20 retained seasons. Four disposable report entries share a 2 MiB serialized bound. Hitting a source bound qualifies counts as a subset. Verified compact league/roster/transaction sections reuse the existing season-cache identity, decode once cold, and reject an interrupted publication before caching.
- Complete means acquired expected buckets in the **listed** seasons, not the entire lifetime of the league. Missing/corrupt/unacquired evidence is not observed zero. Completed events with creation-only, invalid or future clocks do not establish dated activity. Season counts are facts, not predictive trends.
- Season-observed manager counts cover the selected franchise only. Unique historical season-owner evidence is reused; conflicting/unknown owners, current snapshots, pick origins and franchise renames do not backdate tenure. Exact intra-season handovers and behavior at a different franchise remain unestablished. No personal preference is inferred from current roster strength/age.
- Acquisition directions are counted once per position and trade using supported current player identity, explicitly not historical slot eligibility. Historical youth/veteran preference and historical Market fairness are not inferred. Existing compatible historical quote admission and FOIS retained grades remain unchanged.
- Full League/Team/Trade reads prepare the shared report; ordinary Home only fingerprints source revisions and consumes prepared card explanations. A changed source invalidates incompatible context even when transaction count is unchanged. Retrieval-only fields and byte-identical canonical republication do not change factual identity. Prepared source checksums are reused without archive decoding; unverified file changes remain conservative invalidation guards. No provider fetch, durable report or read/unread store is added.
- Partner explanations join the observed franchise facts to existing **current, complete, non-declining package** needs. Unknown/stale/conditional diagnostics cannot create a positive history/need tie-break. Only matches backed by sufficient current-manager season-owner evidence break otherwise equivalent results; prior-owner or unattributed franchise patterns stay descriptive and neutral. Such matches only break otherwise equivalent results after existing strategy, production, Market and plausibility dimensions. No recommendation labels or numerical engines change.
- League consumes the existing authenticated league/franchise strategy cookie, without saving or inventing preferences. Incomplete original consideration is qualified beside primary counts, so unsupported picks cannot appear to be complete zeros. Trade source links use the shared 44 px actions. League uses the existing manager-read worker boundary so cold archive preparation cannot monopolize the event loop. Team insights follow the roster and positional evidence. Default League franchise rows and technical coverage are closed; Home gains no diagnostic panel. Trade retained assessments label their partner context NOT REVALIDATED.
