# DTOS Navigation Model

DTOS navigation follows the General Manager's workflow:

1. **Home** — read the Commissioner Desk briefing.
2. **Team HQ** — understand franchise direction and roster construction.
3. **Trade Center** — act on the highest-value roster opportunity.
4. **Matchups** — prepare for weekly competition.
5. **Transactions** — review what changed across the league.
6. **Draft Capital** — inspect future flexibility.
7. **History** — understand evidence across seasons.
8. **Front Office** — understand observable franchise behavior.
9. **Settings** — review league configuration and synchronization state.

The Commissioner Desk reads as an executive briefing: What changed, What should I
do, My Team, League Opportunities, Around the League, League Intelligence, then
League Snapshot. Unfinished sections stay out of the primary flow.

API endpoints remain discoverable through OpenAPI and inspection contracts rather
than occupying primary navigation.
