# Recommended Trades: bounded evidence policy

Recommended Trades discovers hypotheses, not independent trade grades. Every
constructed package is assessed by the accepted bilateral evaluator. No change
to recommendation, plausibility, missing-evidence or capacity gates is permitted
to populate an otherwise empty result list.

## Discovery and dynamic need

Discovery reads the compatible prepared league-specific weekly optimal lineups.
A candidate must improve an eligible supported slot in at least one supported
week; a complementary outgoing player must independently have supported slot
utility for the other team. This is an opportunity hypothesis, never a claim
that either player is expendable or that the net trade improves both teams.

Position-diverse shortlists and a six-thesis round-robin budget bound normal
discovery. There are no position-count need/surplus thresholds, generic position
scores or roster-size-as-quality rules. This is explicitly a bounded player
lineup search, not exhaustive future-pick/dynasty opportunity coverage.

Removal effects, replacement quality, reserve/depth changes across supported
weeks, known byes, configured roster capacity and compatible Competitive Window
context are assessed on the complete package by the shared evaluator. A deep
position is not automatically called surplus. Unsupported context remains
unavailable. Future picks are not weekly production.

## Reasons and timing

- Roster Construction: supported positive legal-lineup effects, scoped by horizon.
- Natural Trade Partner: supported positive effects on both sides, not willingness.
- Win-Now Opportunity: near-term gain plus matching-generation canonical contender
  context. No independent contender classification.
- Value Opportunity: fully supported canonical acquisition-price advantage, not
  intrinsic bargain, historical movement or timing urgency.
- Future Value / Sell-High: withheld unless their distinct conclusions are
  supported. Generic pick receipts or a price edge alone cannot establish them.

Stable hypothetical lineup gains are reported separately from Why Now. Supported
playoff-window fit, current Competitive Window alignment and known near-term
bye/lineup fit can provide current context without claiming a historical change.
A bye explanation does not causally attribute the entire trade effect to a bye.

No Market/projection/role/pick movement is inferred from retrieval dates, different
projection weeks, incompatible scales, FOIS activity or a current range alone.
When no supported current catalyst is available, Why Now remains unavailable;
this does not invalidate an otherwise qualifying stable opportunity.

## Ranking, refresh and safety

Deterministic canonical recommendation, plausibility, confidence and scoped
horizon effects order qualifying results. Canonical assessment is not altered
by filters. Refresh excludes material base-exchange families before discovery;
nominal pick sweeteners do not create new families. Exclusions are page-session
local, league-bound and capped at 64. No random shuffle or durable discovery
history is used. New sessions start without previous exclusions.

Prepared projection weeks and pre-trade strength are reused within a pinned
search. Start/end generation checks reject changed canonical context. Search
does not prepare Market/FOIS/Picks or mutate ownership, rosters or intelligence.

## Bounded recall

Acceptance examines at most six additional supported omitted theses, prioritizing
counterparties absent from the normal budget, with at most three packages each.
Every diagnostic package uses the same shared evaluator and eligibility gate.
No qualifying result means only that this bounded recall found none; it is not
a claim that no possible trade exists. Do not widen indefinitely to force offers.
