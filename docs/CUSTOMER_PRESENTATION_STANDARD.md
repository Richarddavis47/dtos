## Three layers

1. Answer/action: identify the team, player, transaction or offer; show the strongest supported fact and one primary action. No unsupported model tiles or repeated empty labels.
2. Explanation: say why the fact matters and its principal cost or limitation. Keep material uncertainty adjacent to its claim, once. A provisional or retained assessment stays qualified. Missing preparation is not a weakness, an empty acquisition is not zero activity, and an optional offer is not endorsed.
3. Evidence: existing native details/summary, closed initially, labelled Calculations and sources or a more specific heading. Keep exact original reasons, clocks, identities, provenance, methodological boundaries and JSON exports accessible here. Do not remove evidence or recalculate it to render a disclosure.

## Vocabulary and evidence

Use Market value, personal value, official standings, projected lineup comparison and manager grade as distinct terms. Normal copy says both teams instead of bilateral; different eligible backups instead of disjoint reserve cover; league/week/scoring explanation instead of compatible canonical prepared publication. Do not blindly replace strings across HTML, source/API contracts or verdicts. Preserve RETAINED / NOT REVALIDATED exactly. Sleeper alone supplies fantasy projections/results. Unknown/zero/negative and source precision are unchanged.

A display limitation, an unimplemented model, genuine missing data and preparation pending are different states. Render supported independent facts and only name missing slots/reasons supplied by diagnostics. Show actual dates for sparse observations. Human time labels use an explicit known zone; an absent timezone remains unknown. Exact original timestamps remain in source detail. No unsupported freshness inference.

## Layout and interaction

Keep the existing charcoal/green tokens and Home/My Team/Trade/League/Market navigation. Use readable body text at existing size or larger, 44px controls/focusable summaries, wrapping exact picks/names and no horizontal page overflow. Do not shrink text to shorten cards. Group related facts, use native accessible disclosures, and retain focus/contextual Back/filter/pagination/drafts/comparison/protections. Specialist tools stay secondary. Loading, capacity deferral, retained previous results and completed zero-result searches remain distinct. Ordinary navigation/disclosures must not acquire providers, sync, evaluate or search.

## Trade

Collapsed offer: original decision; partner/exact sent/received; decisive reason and principal cost/risk; concise qualified two-sided lineup effect when supported; active valuation and acquisition roles; one Review trade action. All detailed evidence belongs in the existing prepared offer review: summary, your/their team, independent pre/post lineups, depth/roster space, draft capital, sources. Comparison and Make It Cheaper remain existing explicit actions; no new evaluator or search. Lowest-cost/best-fit are preserved, different extras remain visible, FAIR / OPTIONAL is not promoted.

## Evidence and validation

Before/after rendered fixtures are separate from live owner observations. Use the same source fixture and viewport; measure default card height/text and visually inspect screenshots. Source-to-screen findings must record trace, classification, matched control and resolution; do not claim a data-path fix from rewording. Every material changed state gets regression coverage; tests whose presentation contract changes must preserve their original numerical, authorization, resource and workflow assertions.
