# Sleeper fantasy results (build 2234)

Fantasy-point projections and actual results exclusively use Sleeper. This changes the actual-results source contract; it does not change league scoring, Market formulas, optimizer policy or Trade qualification.

## Confirmed source-to-screen defects

The prior player-production adapter reconstructed fantasy scores from NFLverse game statistics. Applicable missing special-teams statistics could therefore withhold a fantasy average even when the retained Sleeper league matchup payload explicitly supplied the player's score. Its dossier, selected Market asset, current-production layer, asset evidence and Trade fact consumers inherited this restriction. The old unused CachedProductionProvider also labeled arbitrary, unscoped cached numbers as Sleeper statistics. Matchup reads silently preferred ordered starter scores over contradictory player-map scores, and synchronization rounded player results before retaining them in its display groups.

The canonical forward-projection publication already uses Sleeper projected statistics, the selected league's actual scoring coefficients, exact season/week/generation guards and Sleeper web display compatibility. Those paths, legal-lineup calculations and source-order display formatting are preserved. Dormant calibration utilities are not part of the authoritative projection publication. No new provider fallback is admitted.

## Shared actual contract

`src/core/player_value_projection/sleeper_actuals.py` interprets existing `season_matchups` rows. `players_points` and correctly indexed `starters_points` are explicit Sleeper score observations; neither has silent precedence. Finite JSON numbers, including zero and negative values, remain at source precision. Null, invalid, absent and placeholder records do not supply a score. A valid alternate may supply a score when the other field is null/invalid, with that qualification disclosed. Different valid scores, repeated starter identities or conflicting ownership observations withhold the dependent value. Identical duplicate roster observations count once per league/season/player/week. Team `custom_points` never becomes individual production.

No current roster, taxi/IR membership, free-agent identity, bye, DNP or missing week implies a player score or game played. A map explicitly supplying a score is evidence even when roster-role classification is absent. Windows average completed-week score observations, not games played, and display observed totals rather than claiming complete season acquisition. A dependent window containing a missing/conflicting observed score is withheld; independent supported windows and individual weekly scores remain visible. Live/unconfirmed weeks are separate. Future placeholder rows are excluded. Calendar labels distinguish league regular-season/playoff weeks, not NFL postseason acquisition. An earlier league-season's results require their own admitted Sleeper publication and are not substituted from NFLverse.

Each publication carries league, season, selected scoring identity, source generation and retrieval clocks. New native weekly acquisition retains per-week observation/attempt times, explicit failed acquisition, compatible last-valid rows, and detected score corrections. A failed week is never relabeled successful. Known matching old atomic caches can be interpreted from native Sleeper rows only when league/season, saved league scoring, calendar reference, source generation and observation clock agree. This is explicitly qualified as retained cache recovery, not new upstream verification. Missing/incompatible provenance remains unavailable. This never relabels an older NFLverse calculation.

Actuals are derived during existing admitted synchronization or saved-runtime restoration. Rendering performs no provider call, historical import, deep sizing, synchronization or new worker work. The existing atomic runtime cache owns bounded retention; no separate provider database is introduced.

## Consumers and independent analysis

Dossier and selected Market panels show windows, per-week scores, observed coverage, completion state, acquisition gaps, retrieval time and limitations. The Market current-production layer and Trade facts use the same admitted actual contract. Trade facts explicitly label their observation denominator and leave games-played/PPG fields unset. Existing current-context guards withhold results after league/scoring/source-generation mismatch.

NFLverse targets/carries and other usage remain separately labeled analysis with their own samples. The fixed-reference valuation analysis, reference generation and formulas remain unchanged; they are not authoritative fantasy results. `canonical_player_evidence` explicitly separates Sleeper production from `non_sleeper_analysis` and analysis source-check metadata. Retained historical grades remain RETAINED / NOT REVALIDATED; no existing historical record is rewritten or represented as newly Sleeper-validated. New native history ingestion uses the same conflict-aware player-score resolver. Public Career History summaries also leave unverified games-played/PPG fields unset, expose score-observation counts and supported subtotals, and withhold dependent averages for incomplete samples. Non-Sleeper retained calculations cannot fill those actual-result fields. The legacy aggregate used internally by retained grading models is unchanged.

## Evidence limits

The controls use disposable native-shaped Sleeper fixtures and actual preparation, sync, restoration and rendered routes. Their expected individual scores are the retained fixture values, not scores reconstructed by the new implementation. Fixture values are not historical Granite parity observations. No owner-authenticated live score parity is claimed without an independently authorized, compatible live observation. No production sync, Trade search, FOIS rebuild, cache clearing, restart or preference mutation is authorized or performed by these controls.
