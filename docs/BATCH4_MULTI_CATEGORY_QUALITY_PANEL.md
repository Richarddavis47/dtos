## Method

Trading classifications are ordinal conclusions from the corrected historical evaluator, not continuous measurements. Equal-decision central classifications summarize only actual supported conclusions. Missing magnitudes and descriptive partial dimensions receive no default magnitude. The existing minimum trade sample of three is used; smaller samples retain their individual distributions without a category conclusion. Even samples retain both central labels if different. This resists isolated outliers without rewarding activity or weighting a large package from an unverified importance proxy.

This is a diagnostic summary, not a replacement production scoring engine. No academic letter grade is inferred from an ordinal median. Results continue using the accepted Results scorer. Confidence below refers to the supported Trading sample, not to outcome probability or all historical decisions.
