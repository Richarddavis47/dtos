## What changed

Completed Franchise History trade details can show the original recipient’s next supported outgoing completed exchange. An exact received pick can be followed through an observed selection into that selected player’s next trade. The later deal appears once when several original receipts share it. The recipient’s side leads, with the followed asset distinguished from other consideration. The full return belongs to the package, never to an individual component. Partial consideration keeps supported movements visible and withholds a full-return claim.

Existing subsequent movements, current-owner observations, individual pre-trade Market quotes, recipient scoring observations and RETAINED / NOT REVALIDATED grades remain separate. There is no allocated value/production, historical winner, intent inference or championship credit.
