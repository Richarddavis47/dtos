## What the sources establish

- Sleeper distinguishes official NFL injured reserve from the weekly Out tag. Official NFL IR can qualify for a fantasy IR spot. Out alone is governed by league settings. [Sleeper IR guide](https://support.sleeper.com/en/articles/1983643-how-does-injured-reserve-ir-work) · [Gameday designations](https://support.sleeper.com/en/articles/3570017-injury-statuses-and-ir-eligibility)
- Sleeper’s draft-pick documentation shows David Johnson with `status: "Injured Reserve"` and `injury_status: "Out"`. A live read of the documented draft endpoint returned exactly those two fields. Its player news timestamp is December 2017, and it is an archived draft record. The current catalog row for the same Sleeper player ID has no IR injury designation. [Sleeper draft API documentation](https://docs.sleeper.com/#get-all-picks-in-a-draft) · [Retained raw field excerpt](historic-draft-native-excerpt.json)
- The fresh catalog capture is from the exact endpoint DTOS uses. It returned 12,229 rows and no IR/Out pair in either orientation, including the abbreviated and full IR values. All 227 rows with the general `status: "Injured Reserve"` had no NFL team in that snapshot. Contemporary examples show a generic Inactive status with an explicit IR injury designation, and a generic Active status with an Out designation. Catalog retrieval and caching metadata do not date a player’s status transition. [Catalog endpoint](https://api.sleeper.app/v1/players/nfl) · [Receipt and attributable excerpts](native-catalog-receipt.json)
