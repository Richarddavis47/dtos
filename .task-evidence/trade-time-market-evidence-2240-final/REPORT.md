## Root cause and correction

A production-source checkout reproduces two assertion failures without setup errors: a newer incompatible quote masks an older compatible one, and a newer foreign-scope quote does likewise. The old shared reader chose by time first; the history consumer rejected the winner without trying an older eligible state.

The shared read method now accepts an optional pure eligibility predicate and applies it before choosing latest. Trade uses the extracted existing semantic and exact-identity/scope checks, rejects explicit unknown or later knowledge/publication clocks, and retains all known timestamp/format/method restrictions. Default callers retain their original temporal contract. The exact asset and one/two existing asset-type contexts are queried in one indexed streaming read, with constant candidate state and no provider call or heavyweight lease. Unresolved same-instant price conflicts, including pick aliases, stay unknown. Unknown metadata remains unavailable. Foreign raw evidence is not exposed. Store writers, schemas and immutable observations are unchanged.

The corrected fixture retains a 400-valued compatible quote despite the newer bad or foreign 800-valued row. It is not a change to normalization or a claim that a real asset costs 400. Partial packages keep supported individual prices; zero stays numeric, and subsecond age does not become an exact trade instant. Exact year/round/original-franchise picks never borrow the converted player's price.
