## What changes

“Your draft capital after this trade” groups owned picks before→after by the seasons retained in the admitted ledger and the league's configured rounds. Own firsts are separate from firsts originating with other franchises. Native expandable disclosures list exact owned picks and exact sent/acquired identities, including original franchise and canonical ID.

Complete counts require agreement among the existing canonical ledger, native Sleeper transfers and all team-owned pick views. Unique valid identities must cover the retained season/configured-round/franchise domain. No missing season or pick is created. Missing/partial evidence withholds remaining totals while preserving independently verified proposed movements. Conflicting or ambiguous identities are withheld. Only a complete inventory can establish zero.

Each assessment freezes a bounded ownership snapshot independent of projection and price qualifications. Comparison uses each offer's recorded baseline; keeping the roster has a common capital baseline only when recorded complete ownership generations agree. Retained results are NOT REVALIDATED. Missing legacy snapshots, oversized records and incompatible contexts do not borrow today's inventory. No new provider call, evaluation, synchronization or background worker runs from opening/expanding/comparing the view.
