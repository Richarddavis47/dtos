## Confirmed gap and correction

Baseline cards named the partner without a direct history link. Evaluate offered only sampled partner trade references in an optional disclosure, and Team HQ was a separate multi-hop path. Unselected recommendation results were page-local; a full return could not guarantee retaining the clicked offer. The narrow correction adds “See franchise trade history” after safely resolving the exact unique partner in the active league and workspace. Unknown, malformed, self, duplicate and foreign contexts omit the link. Existing History authorization rechecks league scope; an opaque workspace binding never grants access.

The clicked recommendation is retained through the existing bounded preview mechanism. It is not adopted and the original draft stays unchanged. Evaluate retains its draft/current assessment. Mode, temporary strategy, applicable filters, original asset/pick IDs and source qualifications survive contextual Back. Existing not-revalidated/freshness rules still apply; history does not refresh a recommendation. An account or league switch is reauthorized and invalidates stale return context. Modified/new-tab link activation does not rewrite the original draft.

No new endpoint, provider read, backfill, sync, search, historical metric or durable recommendation storage. History remains descriptive; current manager intent, counterparty acceptance, Trade/FOIS scores and valuation formulas do not use these links. The 2GiB cap, restoration16× allowance, FOIS768MiB reserve and Trade256+128MiB policy remain intact. Submitted Sleeper lineups and saved user settings are unchanged.
